## VRmin 0.47

Big update for VR games: **DOOM VFR**, **Google Earth VR** and **Half-Life 2: VR Mod** now play on your Mac.

### New games working
- **Google Earth VR**: it renders, your controllers show up and the buttons work. Turning with the thumbsticks doesn't work, but that also happens on Windows.
- **DOOM VFR**: it now renders and is playable (the Mac's GPU didn't support some of the graphics features it uses, so VRmin translates them).
- **Half-Life 2: VR Mod**: 32-bit VR games are now supported. VRmin downloads a newer OpenComposite build (GameNative's v10) by itself the first time. Menus and the HUD are shown in the headset, sharp, and you can point and click with the controller. While a level loads, the headset shows "Loading…" instead of going back to the room.

### Better for every Vulkan game
- **No more shaking when you move your head** in Vulkan games (DOOM VFR, Half-Life 2: VR Mod). VRmin now tells the headset the exact head position each image was drawn with.
- **Less latency**: images are read from the GPU without slowing the game down, and on the Mac they go to the GPU without extra copies.

### Other changes
- New option in VRmin Host's advanced settings: **Emulate HTC Vive controllers**, for games made for Vive wands (works like the Index option).
- **Force quit** now also closes games that were opened directly instead of through Steam.
- **VRmin Host quits completely** when you close it (it no longer stays running in the background).
- **Quest app 0.71**: **Quit VRmin** from Studio mode now really closes the app, and the Vive controllers option is in the menu too.
- Apps that come with VR files but aren't tagged as VR on Steam (like Google Earth VR) show up in the VR games list (from 0.46 beta).

### Downloads

- `VRmin-0.71.apk`: the Quest app
