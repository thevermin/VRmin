## VRmin 0.53

VRmin for Windows catches up with the Mac: the same OpenComposite, per-game quality, the headset's microphone in games, and quality presets for your graphics card.

### VRmin for Windows 0.3 (beta)
- **A full VRmin Host window, like on the Mac:** connection status, first-time setup in numbered steps, Steam (open, show, close), your games with their covers (Play, Force quit, whether each one works, and "Did it work?" after trying a new one), each game's own quality and performance, Settings (quality presets for your graphics card, microphone, performance, language, open with Windows, don't sleep while the Quest uses the PC), Help and the log. It uses WebView2, which comes with Windows 10 and 11.
- **OpenComposite built by VRmin** now comes inside the Windows app too, instead of being downloaded. Newer SteamVR games that closed right away now open, and each eye's image goes to VRmin without an extra copy.
- **Menus no longer show mirrored** in games like Kayak VR: Mirage, and menus that don't change aren't read again every frame (same fixes as on the Mac).
- **Sentinel Mode:** play with the headset resting on your desk while the game shows in a window on your PC, like on the Mac. With the Quest in developer mode and connected over USB-C, VRmin Host keeps the headset's display on and can open VRmin on it for you (**Start**, in "Headset over USB-C cable"). Following your head with the PC's camera is still Mac only.
- **Updates itself:** VRmin Host for Windows tells you when there's a new version and installs it with one click (it closes and the new one opens). You can also get beta versions, in Settings.
- **Installs the Quest app over the cable:** with the Quest connected over USB-C (developer mode), "Headset over USB-C cable" shows the version on the headset and installs or updates VRmin without SideQuest.
- **Record a video:** "Record" in VRmin Host (or in the headset's quick settings) records the PC's screen and, with the headset on the cable, what you see in the headset. Videos go to Videos › VRmin, without sound, like on the Mac.
- **3D with AI:** the desktop and each window can be shown in 3D, like on the Mac. An AI (Depth Anything V2) works out the depth of each image on your graphics card (DirectML); it downloads the first time you turn it on (about 50 MB).
- **Separate windows:** open any window of your PC (or any of its monitors) in its own screen in the headset, use it with the laser or your hands, and resize it from the headset, like on the Mac.
- **Switch monitor:** with more than one monitor, the "Switch monitor" button on the headset's main screen shows the next one, like on the Mac.
- **Each game can have its own quality.** Pick it from the game's menu in the headset; it applies while that game runs.
- **The headset's microphone in games.** Install [VB-CABLE](https://vb-audio.com/Cable/) (free), then click **Use the headset's microphone** in VRmin Host. While you play, Windows' microphone becomes the headset's; when you stop, yours comes back.
- **Something's wrong** button: saves a report on the Desktop and opens the page to send it in an Issue. Asking for a report from the headset does the same.

### Quest app
- **Quality presets for PCs.** When the Quest is connected to a PC, Low, Medium, High and Ultra describe which graphics cards they're meant for, and the one for your card is marked as recommended.

### Mac
- No changes since 0.52; the version number moves up so the Mac and Windows apps stay together.
