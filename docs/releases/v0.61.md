## VRmin 0.61

### Mac: real SteamVR
- **Games draw at VRmin's resolution with real SteamVR**, the same as with OpenComposite: your quality setting and each game's own settings in VRmin Host. SteamVR's automatic resolution never goes below the first headsets' on a Mac, which was more than twice the pixels (Half-Life 2: VR Mod was very slow).
- **New setting: SteamVR chooses the resolution.** Turn it on in VRmin Host's settings to use SteamVR's own resolution setting instead (Settings › Video in its menu).
- **Fixed: the image shook when turning your head** in Vulkan games like Half-Life 2: VR Mod. SteamVR got the previous frame with your new head position.
- **SteamVR shows your Quest's controllers**: Touch Plus on Quest 3 and 3S, Touch Pro on Quest Pro.
- **SteamVR keeps its pace better**: VRmin no longer makes SteamVR's compositor wait for the GPU.

### Mac: VRmin Host
- **The Store lists every VR game in your Steam library**, not only the ones you had opened on this Mac. The first time it takes a few minutes to check which ones are VR.
- **Fixed: two VRmin Host windows** could open at the same time.
- **The Windows engine has an update**: VRmin asks you to complete it once, with one click (your games and Steam stay as they are).

### Quest app 0.87
- **Real hands in the game** now shows your hands with the headset's passthrough when VRmin's camera has no images (before, nothing showed).
