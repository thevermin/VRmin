## VRmin 0.57

### Mac
- **Fixed: installing the engine ended with an error (code 1) after updating to 0.56.** When the Windows inside VRmin was updated, Steam opened by itself, and closing it made the installer stop with an error. Now Steam is closed and the installation continues.
  If this happened to you: install this version, open VRmin Host and click **Install** on the engine again. Steam and your games are kept.
- **New "Reinstall the engine" button**, under "Something's wrong". If the engine won't install or isn't working right, it downloads and installs it again. Steam, your games and your saves are kept.
