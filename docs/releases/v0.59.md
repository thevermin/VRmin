## VRmin 0.59

### Mac: your own library and store, inside VRmin Host
VRmin Host now has **Home**, **Library** and **Store** tabs. Steam keeps running in the background, and you only see its window when you sign in. If you prefer Steam's own window, choose it in Settings › Game library.
- **Library**: your games with their art, split into **VR** and **Flat** tabs. **All** also shows the games in your account that aren't installed yet. The side list folds down to just the icons, and everything fits any window size.
- **Each game has its own page**: play it in the Quest, or **on the Mac** without the headset. You can also pick its VR system and quality, see when you last played it and how much space it uses, and open its logs, diagnostics or uninstall it.
- **Downloads without Steam's window**: press **Install** and the game downloads in the background, like in GameHub. The amber bar at the bottom shows the progress, speed and time left. You can **pause, resume or cancel** a download from there.
- **Store**: search Steam's store, and see the VR games in your account that you haven't installed.
- **Games from outside Steam**: **Add a game** in the Library list lets you pick any Windows `.exe`.
- **Steam account**: sign out from Settings. When you're signed out, Home shows a button to sign in.

### Mac: fixes for The Lab and other older SteamVR games
- **Fixed: some games opened the wrong program** (The Lab opened one of its small extra programs instead of the game). VRmin now checks each game's files again after updating.
- **Fixed: "SteamVR took too long to open"** when SteamVR had turned VRmin's driver off in its safe mode. VRmin turns it back on.
- **Automatic VR system**: games made for other controllers (The Lab, DOOM VFR and others without Quest controls) open with real SteamVR when it's installed.
- **Real SteamVR now knows how fast your hands and head move.** Games that use it work properly now. In SUPERHOT VR, time moves when you move your arms, and punches hit.
- **Reports now include SteamVR's logs**, so problems with real SteamVR are easier to find.

### Mac: more
- **Install SteamVR** with one button (Library › SteamVR).
- **A warning when a game or the server closes by itself**, with a button to make a report.
- **A warning when the Mac runs out of memory** during a game, naming the apps that use the most.
- **Wi-Fi check** in Settings, with tips for playing.
- **Reinstall everything, keeping your games** (Settings): starts the engine and Steam over from scratch, and keeps your games and saves.
- **The game video always uses the Mac's hardware encoder.** If one codec can't, VRmin tries the other one before falling back to the CPU.

### Quest app 0.85
- **SGSR upscaling for VR games** (Settings › Image): sharper game video in the headset. It needs foveated encoding turned off.
- **Fixed: sharpening could mix the two eyes** at the edge between them.

### Linux 0.2 (beta)
- **Updates by itself** from GitHub, like on Windows.
