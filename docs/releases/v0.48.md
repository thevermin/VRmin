## VRmin 0.48

A new loading screen, smoother room changes, and Half-Life 2: VR Mod working out of the box.

### Loading screen
- When you open a game (from the headset or from VRmin Host), the headset moves to a dark, calm loading space. A panel shows the game's cover, its name and what's happening (opening Steam, opening the game, entering VR).
- Tips appear in random order and change every 5 seconds; use the arrow to see the next one.
- **Back to the room** takes you back to your room without stopping the game.
- When a game closes, you see a short notice before going back to your room.
- The loading space is also available as a room of its own: **Loading**, in Rooms.

### Rooms
- Changing rooms now goes straight from one room to the next with the same transition as entering VR.
- Recentering with the Meta button now brings the room right in front of you.

### Half-Life 2: VR Mod
- Works out of the box: VRmin now opens it with the right settings by itself (it was marked as not working in 0.47).
- You can grab objects with the grip (bottles, ammo from your shoulder…). The buttons are the same as on Windows with a Quest.
- Less latency: frames are no longer queued up before reaching the headset.
- Smoother when new things appear on screen.
- The "Loading…" message between levels is no longer doubled.

### DOOM VFR
- With **Emulate HTC Vive controllers**:
  - pushing the stick to the edge is like pressing the trackpad in that direction, so menus and dash work without clicking the stick;
  - A/X press the center of the trackpad and B/Y act as the menu button;
  - both hands are tilted so weapons point the right way;
  - you can turn with the right stick (30° per push).

### Other changes
- Hand tilt and roll adjustments (game menu › Hands and laser) are now saved per game.
- In games that use OpenComposite, the Touch grip now works as "grab" where the game expects it.
- Fixed the engine install on some Apple Silicon Macs ("dylib not compatible, needs arm64e", [#10](https://github.com/thevermin/VRmin/issues/10)).
- Fixed the Windows runtime build that made Half-Life 2 show as a flat screen.
