## VRmin 0.49

A fix for new installs, a much bigger cinema, a new Space room and a first beta for Windows.

### Important fix
- Installing VRmin on a Mac for the first time failed while installing the Windows games engine ("d3d11.dll: No such file or directory"). It works again. If it happened to you: update, then click **Install** next to the Windows games engine again.

### Rooms
- **Cinema**: a much bigger room with rows of seats, aisles, a stage, wall lights and more detail. The screen is bigger and higher, and walking up and down the rows feels smooth.
- **Space**: no floor, you just float. A real round planet with clouds and a ring that slowly tilts around it, with the planet's shadow on the ring and the ring's shadow on the planet. There's also a moon, a spiral galaxy, a far-away sun, colorful nebulae and shooting stars now and then.
- The sky no longer turns with you when you turn.
- Changing rooms always puts you back in the middle of the new room.

### Half-Life 2: VR Mod
- The "Loading…" message between levels is farther away and looks right in both eyes.
- Smoother when new things show up on screen.

### Other changes
- Game frames are handled at the highest priority on the Mac.
- The logs now say when the Mac gets hot and slows down (helps us find performance problems).

### VRmin for Windows (beta)
- A first version of VRmin for Windows PCs: play the Steam VR games on your PC on the Quest, and use the Windows desktop from the headset.
- It hasn't been tested on a real Windows PC yet. If you try it, please tell us how it went in an [Issue](https://github.com/thevermin/VRmin/issues/new/choose) and attach the `servidor.log` file ("Logs" button).
- The steps are in the `LEEME.txt` file inside the .zip.

### Downloads

- `VRmin Windows 0.1.zip`: the Windows app (beta)
