## VRmin 0.50

Simple quality settings, your microphone in games, menus in more games, and a clearer VRmin Host and headset menu.

### Quality made simple
- Pick **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Ultra**. Each one says which Macs it's meant for, and the one for your Mac's chip is marked as recommended.
- **Custom** shows every setting on its own (sharpness, frame rate, image quality, foveated encoding and codec).
- The same choices are in VRmin Host, in the headset's Settings and in the in-game menu. Each game can also have its own quality.

### Microphone in games
- Games can now use the **Quest's microphone** (or a headset with a microphone plugged into the Quest's USB-C port).
- Or choose **any microphone on your Mac**, like a USB or Bluetooth headset.
- Set it in VRmin Host › Settings › **Games microphone**. Using the Quest's microphone needs a small virtual microphone, **VRmin Mic**, installed once from there (macOS asks for your password).
- The headset only records while a game is open.

### Games
- **Menus that were missing now show up** in DirectX 11 games, like Kayak VR: Mirage.
- Newer SteamVR games that closed right away when opening now reach VRmin, thanks to a newer OpenComposite (thanks, Stooovie!).
- Fixed: the headset app could close when a game finished loading (it happened with Beat Saber).
- Tested and working: **The Lab** and **Catto Pew Pew**.

### VRmin Host
- First-time setup is now 3 numbered steps, and it gets out of the way once everything is installed.
- The status at the top fixes what's missing when you click it (for example, it opens Steam).
- **Play** works even before the Quest connects.
- Each game's quality and performance settings are in the settings button on its cover.
- After closing a game you haven't tried before, VRmin Host asks if it worked.
- Settings apply by themselves when you close them.
- The USB-C cable features (Sentinel Mode and installing the Quest app) have their own card.

### In the headset
- Settings are grouped as General, Quality, Comfort and Studio, with separate pages for Hands, Gestures and Laser.
- The portal is now in Rooms.
- Long explanations are behind a **?** button.
- The first tab is now called **Connection**.
- The main screen takes the shape of the Mac monitor you show, with no black bars, also in rooms with a fixed screen.
- The room runs at 90 Hz from the start.
- Smoother animations: panels open with a little bounce, the wrist menu comes out of your watch, a glow when you change rooms, and the game list appears in a cascade.
- If the app ever closes unexpectedly, the reason is saved in the report so it can be fixed.

### VRmin for Windows 0.2 (beta)
- Menus in DirectX 11 games now show up (same fix as on the Mac).
