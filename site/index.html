Your SteamVR games, on a Mac.

VRmin runs the Windows version of your Steam VR games on an Apple Silicon Mac and streams them to your Meta Quest over Wi‑Fi. Free and open source.

Apple Silicon Mac + Meta Quest + Steam · Windows and Linux PC (beta)

What it does

Two apps that work together: VRmin Host on the Mac, and VRmin on the Quest.

SteamVR games on your Mac

Your library on Apple Silicon. VRmin picks the right way to run each game: OpenXR, OpenComposite, or the real SteamVR.

Your Mac in your room

Your whole desktop, or any single window, floating wherever you want.

Your real hands

Hand tracking with your real hands over every window. Touch them directly or point with the laser.

Pick your room

A living room, space, a cinema… or mixed reality with your real desk still there.

Settings inside the game

Quality, sharpness, frame rate and codec, per game, from a menu in VR.

Draw a portal

Draw a circle in the air and see through to your real room.

Sentinel Mode

Put the Quest on your monitor: it tracks you while the game shows on your Mac's screen.

  • Flat games on a giant screen, and side‑by‑side 3D videos
  • Direct GPU copy from the game to the video encoder
  • Foveated encoding: sharper where you look
  • Controller vibration in your games
  • Your Mac's sound in the headset, and the headset keyboard types on your Mac
  • Your microphone in games
  • Simple quality settings, with the right one for your Mac marked
  • Updates by itself, the Quest app too (over USB-C)

Install

The full steps are in the README.

On your Mac

Download VRmin Host, drag it to Applications and open it (right‑click › Open the first time). Then click Install on the engine and on Steam, and download your VR games.

On your Quest

Turn on developer mode and install VRmin with SideQuest, or from VRmin Host over a USB-C cable.

Play

Open VRmin Host on the Mac and VRmin on the Quest, on the same Wi‑Fi. Pick a game and play.

Requirements

MacApple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 14.2 or newer. 16 GB of memory is better for heavy games.
HeadsetMeta Quest 3 or 3S (recommended), Quest 2. Quest 1 and Quest Pro are experimental.
NetworkThe Mac and the Quest on the same router. 5 GHz Wi‑Fi, or the Mac on Ethernet, works best.
SteamA Steam account with your VR games. VRmin installs its own Steam for Windows.
Windows (beta)Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), an NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card.
Linux (beta)Steam with Proton, X11, PulseAudio or PipeWire, WebKitGTK 4.1, and a GPU with VA-API or NVENC.
Many SteamVR games that use DirectX 11 work, but not all of them. See the list of tested games, and if something doesn't work, open an issue.
